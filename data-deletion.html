Data Deletion Instructions

NEROUSCALEAI LLC respects your right to control your personal data. This page explains how you can request the deletion of any data we hold about you.

How to Request Deletion

To request that we delete your personal data, send an email to contact@neruoscaleai.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request", including the email address, username, or account details associated with the data you want removed.

What Happens Next

We will verify your request and delete the associated data from our systems, including any data obtained through connected platforms (such as Facebook/Meta, X, or other third-party APIs), within 30 days. We will confirm by email once the deletion is complete.

What Is Deleted

Deletion requests remove personal data we store about you, such as account identifiers, contact details, and usage or engagement data collected through our apps and integrations. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate business record-keeping, such as billing records.
